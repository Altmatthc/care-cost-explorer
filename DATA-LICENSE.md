## An honest note on what this covers

Individual prices are **facts**, and facts are not copyrightable in the United
States (*Feist Publications v. Rural Telephone Service*, 499 U.S. 340 (1991)).
Every price here comes from a file a hospital is federally required to publish.
Anyone may download those same files and extract the same numbers, and this
licence does not — and could not — prevent that.

What the licence applies to is the **compilation**: the selection of
procedures, the normalisation of payer names, the verification that each file
belongs to the hospital it claims, and the assembled structure. That is a thin
right. This notice does not pretend otherwise.

The genuinely distinctive part is `data/*-history.csv`. Hospitals overwrite
their files in place; no public archive of past versions exists. A record of
what a price *used to be* cannot be reconstructed after the fact by anyone,
under any licence. That protection comes from having recorded it, not from
this document.
